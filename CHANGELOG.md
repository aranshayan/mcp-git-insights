# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial release: repo_summary, git_log, git_show, git_diff, git_blame, changelog.

Also in this first release:

- New `code_owners` tool: authors ranked by commits and lines changed under a path, with their first and last change, optionally since a date. Emails are used to group one person's name spellings and are never returned. ([#2](https://github.com/aranshayan/mcp-git-insights/issues/2))
